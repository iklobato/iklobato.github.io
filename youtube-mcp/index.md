# youtube-mcp

youtube-mcp is a private tool used only by the owner of the
[Por Baixo Do Sistema](https://www.youtube.com/@PorBaixoDoSistema) YouTube channel.

It runs on the owner's own computer and connects an AI assistant to the YouTube Data API
and the YouTube Analytics API, so the owner can:

- read channel and video statistics;
- edit channel and video details;
- upload and schedule videos;
- reply to and moderate comments.

Every change to the channel needs the owner's approval before it runs.
It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
